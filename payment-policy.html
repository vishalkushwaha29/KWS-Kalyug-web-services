Payment Policy

For international clients · Last updated: 26 August 2026

Kalyug Web Services (KWS) — Web Development | Design | Social Media | AI & Digital Solutions

In short: work starts after the agreed advance, and final delivery follows the final payment. Payments are split into milestones so that both the client and KWS are protected throughout the project.

1. Payment terms

To keep projects secure and transparent for both KWS and the client, payment is divided into milestones.

Project size Advance Milestone Final
Small — up to $300 50% before work begins — 50% before final delivery
Medium / Large — above $300 30–40% before starting 30% during development 30–40% before delivery

The exact milestone structure will be mentioned in the project proposal or invoice.

2. Monthly services

For services such as social media management, maintenance, or other recurring work:

  • Payment is required before the monthly service period begins.
  • Each month is treated as a separate service period.
  • Additional work outside the agreed scope may be charged separately.

3. Project start

KWS will begin work after:

  1. The project scope is confirmed.
  2. The client accepts the proposal and terms.
  3. The required advance payment has been received.

4. Final delivery

The final version of the project will be delivered after the outstanding balance has been received.

For websites, this may include final deployment, source files, credentials, domain-related transfer, or other ownership materials where applicable.

5. Revisions

The number of included revisions will be mentioned in the project proposal.

Requests outside the agreed scope may require an additional fee and timeline.

6. Cancellation

If a client cancels a project after work has started, the advance payment covers the work and resources already committed and is generally non-refundable, subject to the specific agreement between KWS and the client.

7. Late payments

If a milestone or final payment is delayed, KWS may temporarily pause project work until the pending payment is received.

Any revised delivery date will depend on the payment delay and the project schedule.

8. Payment methods

KWS will provide the payment method and payment details in the invoice or payment request.

The client is responsible for ensuring that the agreed invoice amount is received by KWS. Any applicable payment-processing or currency-conversion charges should be clarified before payment.

For cross-border service payments received in India, KWS maintains appropriate invoices and contracts and follows applicable banking, tax and foreign-exchange requirements. RBI materials recognise invoices and inward remittances as part of service-export and payment processes.

9. Currency

Project prices may be quoted in USD, EUR, GBP, or another mutually agreed currency.

The final invoice will clearly mention:

  • Project amount
  • Currency
  • Payment milestone
  • Due date
  • Payment method

10. Client agreement before starting

Before starting any project, both parties should agree on the following:

Project scope Total price Timeline Milestones Revisions Payment schedule Final delivery

Any changes should be confirmed in writing.

11. Our position

KWS believes in clear communication and milestone-based payments so that both the client and KWS are protected throughout the project.

Work starts after the agreed advance. Final delivery follows the final payment.

Domestic projects

This policy covers international clients. For projects billed in Indian rupees, the payment terms in our Terms & Conditions apply instead.
