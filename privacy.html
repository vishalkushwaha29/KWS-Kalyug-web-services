Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

This policy explains what information Kalyug Web Services (“we”) collects when you use this website, why we collect it, and what we do with it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

1. What this website collects

This site is a static website. It has no backend server and no database. That means:

  • We do not run tracking pixels, advertising cookies or analytics on this site by default.
  • Nothing you type into a form is transmitted to us automatically.
  • When you submit a form, your browser opens WhatsApp or your own email application with the details pre-filled. You choose whether to press send. Until you do, we never receive the information.

2. Information stored on your own device

The “Build Your Website” calculator saves your selections in your browser's local storage so your estimate survives a page refresh. This data never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time from your browser settings, or by pressing “Clear selection” on the calculator.

3. Information you send us

When you contact us by WhatsApp, email or phone, we receive whatever you choose to share — typically your name, contact details and a description of your project. We use it only to reply to you, prepare a quote and deliver work you have engaged us for.

4. Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and icons from Cloudflare's CDN. Those providers receive your IP address as a normal part of serving those files. We have no other relationship with them and pass them no information about you.

WhatsApp links are handled by WhatsApp (Meta) under their own privacy policy.

5. How long we keep things

Enquiries that do not become projects are deleted within 12 months. Project records are kept for 7 years, as Indian tax and accounting rules require.

6. Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask for corrections, or ask us to delete it. Email kalyugwebservices29@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We share it only with contractors working on your project, and only what they need, under confidentiality terms.

8. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and, for active clients, tell you directly.
