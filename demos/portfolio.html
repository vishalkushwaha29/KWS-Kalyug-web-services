About

I started out doing packaging for a spice exporter in Pune and never quite left the world of small, stubborn businesses. Most of my clients are between five and fifty people, and most of them are the first designer they've ever hired.

That shapes how I work: fewer decks, more prototypes, and a handover document your team can actually use six months later when I'm gone.
